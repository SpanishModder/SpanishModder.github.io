Game

Crewmates finish tasks (E near a glowing pad), report bodies (R), and vote out Impostors.
Impostors hunt the Crew (Q or Space when close and ready), blend in, and win when they equal the Crew's numbers.
Meetings start from a body report or the red button in the Cafeteria. Talk, then vote or skip.
Ghosts keep doing tasks, walk through walls, and chat only with other ghosts.
Move with WASD or arrow keys.
