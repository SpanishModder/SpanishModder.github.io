Privacy Policy

What Data Is Processed

Relievo does not have its own server or database: it is a website that runs entirely in your browser. The location you search for, the area you select, and the generated height map are not sent to or stored on any Relievo server.

External Services That Receive Requests

To function, your browser connects directly (without going through any Relievo server) to the following open data providers:

  • OpenStreetMap — to display map imagery.
  • Nominatim (OpenStreetMap) — for the place search.
  • AWS Open Data (Terrain Tiles) — for the elevation data used to generate the height map.

Each of these services may log your IP address like any standard web request, in accordance with their own privacy policies.
